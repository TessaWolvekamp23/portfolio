About me

I am an 18-year-old Communication & Multimedia Design student at Avans University of Applied Sciences in the Netherlands.

To me, design is about immersive storytelling: creating tactile packaging, playful arcade games, or biophilic urban spaces that invite people into a narrative and encourage them to discover deeper layers of meaning.

Core Disciplines

Design Capabilities

Bridging aesthetic sensitivity and interactive media to shape meaningful narratives.

Brand & Packaging

Building visual identity systems, typography hierarchies, print collateral, and 3D product mock-ups with strong shelf presence and tactile appeal.

Game Design & Pixel Art

Designing empathetic characters, frame-by-frame flight and jump animations, obstacle physics, and rewarding micro-interactions.

Spatial & Civic Concepts

Addressing environmental challenges such as urban heat stress through biophilic design interventions, research, and persuasive visualizations.

Interactive Prototyping

Bringing design systems to life with accessible, responsive layouts using clean HTML, modular CSS variables, and fluid JavaScript logic.
