Burg, Burg. - Museum Game

An app where children can experience and explore a museum through exciting, competitive gameplay and scanning features on their phone.

Role
Front End Designer
Context
Avans CMD Academic Project
Tools Used
Figma, Illustrator
Year
2026
01 / Context & Challenge

Burg, Burg.

Burg Burg develops online content and applications. Websites with character, user-friendly administrative interfaces, and effective online tools.

The client wanted a new feature in their existing app ‘Roald’. They asked for a way to make museum visits more engaging and fun for the guests.

02 / Research & Concept

"Museum Game"

The target audience was children from ages 7 to 12. To get a better view of this group and come up with fitting ideas, I did online research. My conclusion was that these children are competitive and want to fit in with their peers.

This led me to the idea of a competitive game in the museum. The idea is that the children get clues based on art in the museum. Whoever scans the correct artwork first, earns the most points. This makes the museum more interactive and fun for children, and secretly teaches them about the history of the displayed art.

User Journey: How we want to reach our guests and enhance their museum experience.
03 / Final Design

Designing the App

The final Figma design is roughly based on the already existing ‘Roald’ app. I did, however, add more colour to make it more child-friendly. The colours are taken directly from the Burg, Burg. logo, to keep the theme alive and fitting.
Click here for a preview.

04 / Reflection & Learning

Key Takeaways

  • Gamified Learning: Transforming museum exploration into a competitive scanning game encourages children to engage with artworks while learning about their historical significance.
  • Target Audience Engagement: Designing around the competitive nature and social motivations of children aged 7–12 helps create an experience that feels playful, interactive, and age-appropriate.
  • Visual Brand Integration: Incorporating Burg, Burg.'s existing brand colours into the Roald app creates a more child-friendly interface while maintaining consistency with the client's visual identity.
