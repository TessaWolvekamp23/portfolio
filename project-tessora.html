Packaging & Advertising Case Study

Tessora — Taste The Temptation

A vibrant beverage brand identity exploring tactile fruit splash dynamics on a 3D aluminum can wrap, paired with a glamorous outdoor urban billboard campaign.

Role
Visual & Packaging Designer
Context
Avans CMD Academic Project
Tools Used
Illustrator, Photoshop, 3D Can Mockup
Year
2026
01 / Context & Challenge

Reinventing the Fruit Soda Experience

Commercial soft drinks frequently lean toward loud neon colors or hyper-minimalist corporate styles. The challenge for Tessora was to bridge the gap: creating a fruit soda packaging experience that feels enticing, luscious, and slightly vintage, while keeping strong visual shelf presence in modern retail environments.

The brief required designing a cohesive visual system that could seamlessly scale from the curved 3D surface of a 330ml aluminum can to large-scale outdoor city advertisements.

02 / Concept & Flavor Dynamics

"Taste The Temptation"

The core concept centers on temptation and indulgence. Rather than relying solely on flat graphic patterns, I explored dynamic liquid physics: rich dark cherries splashing into sparkling turquoise waters, contrasted by melting caramel and chocolate drips at the crown of the can.

For the brand logotype Tessora, I crafted fluid, melting lettering with soft serif flourishes that visually convey dripping syrup and fizzing refreshment.

Outdoor Advertising Campaign: City billboard mock-up showcasing the "Taste The Temptation" campaign, highlighting the brand's glamorous illustrated persona in public urban spaces.
03 / Campaign & Character

Bringing the Brand to the Streets

To accompany the physical packaging, I developed an outdoor street advertising campaign. The billboard features an illustrated muse in a dramatic scarlet gown and wide-brimmed hat, subtly gazing over her shoulder. This visual metaphor reinforces the narrative of indulgence and allure.

The billboard design establishes strong contrast against natural outdoor foliage, ensuring legibility for pedestrians, cyclists, and commuters passing by city transit stops.

04 / Reflection & Learning

Key Takeaways

  • 3D Surface Distortion: Designing for cylindrical aluminum cans requires careful calibration of visual weight so that key branding and typography remain readable from multiple viewing angles.
  • Sensory Storytelling: Combining textural elements (condensation droplets, glossy fruit skins, fluid drips) elevates consumer appetite appeal.
  • Multi-Channel Consistency: Ensuring that the brand's identity translates naturally from handheld packaging to large-format urban billboards.
