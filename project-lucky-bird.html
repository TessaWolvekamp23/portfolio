Game Design & Pixel Art Case Study

Lucky Bird — 2D Arcade Game

A vibrant retro arcade experience featuring custom character design, frame-by-frame pixel sprite animation, and obstacle-dodging gameplay mechanics.

Role
Game Designer & Pixel Artist
Context
Avans CMD Game Prototyping
Tools Used

01 / Concept & Gameplay

Navigating a World of Reaching Hands

Lucky Bird is a fast-paced 2D arcade game built around the quirky humor of being a beloved pet bird. The player navigates an agile flying bird through a narrow domestic hallway, dodging human hands that constantly reach down and up attempting to grab or pet the bird.

To survive and increase their score, the player must swoop past grasping fingers to collect tasty seed clusters and millet sprays scattered across the flight path.

02 / Character Roster & Silhouette Design

Four Playable Avian Personalities

To give players personal expression and replay value, I illustrated four distinct bird species with unique pixel color palettes and distinctive silhouettes:

  • Blue & Gold Macaw: Rich azure plumage with vibrant yellow chest feathers.
  • Peach-faced Lovebird: Lively lime green body with warm coral-peach facial plumage.
  • Blue-footed Booby: Quirky cream and mocha body with signature bright cyan feet.
  • Scarlet Macaw: Fiery crimson body accented with primary yellow and cobalt wingtips.
Avian Roster Lineup: Character selection screen showcasing the four pixel-art bird species alongside the seed cluster reward and obstacle hand asset.
03 / Interactive Sprite Animation

8-Frame Flight Cycle in Action

Every movement in Lucky Bird was created pixel-by-pixel. The flight cycle uses 8 discrete frames to communicate upward flap lift, wing extension, glide momentum, and aerodynamic tuck.

04 / Reflection & Learning

Key Takeaways

  • Pixel Consistency: Maintaining a strict pixel scale (consistent texel size across characters, obstacles, and backgrounds) prevents visual clashing and preserves classic 16-bit aesthetic purity.
  • Readability Under Motion: Because the bird travels at variable speeds, high-contrast color zones (e.g. coral face on lime body) allow players to track their avatar instinctively without losing focus.
  • Anticipation in Micro-Animations: Adding slight ease-in and anticipation to wing flapping creates a satisfying feeling of weight and buoyancy in flight physics.
